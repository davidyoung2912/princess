Princess Cruises unifies its cabin Room Service and ship-wide OceanNow® delivery under a single 24/7 digital ordering system accessible via the Princess MedallionClass app or stateroom TV across Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess.
Guests can order food, drinks, and sundries delivered directly to their stateroom or anywhere on the ship (pool loungers, Piazza seating, theater lobbies, or open decks) using Medallion location tracking.

1. Delivery Fees & Package Coverage (2026 Rules)

• Princess Plus & Princess Premier: $0 delivery fees—both the one-time OceanNow activation fee and per-delivery Room Service fees are completely waived, and all "Included" food items are $0.
• Princess Standard (Base Fare):
	• Continental Breakfast Hang-Tag (6:00 AM – 11:00 AM): $0 (free morning delivery when ordered via the door hanger before midnight).
	• OceanNow® On-Demand Delivery: $14.99 one-time activation fee per guest for unlimited ship-wide delivery throughout the voyage.
	• Phone-In Stateroom Room Service (without OceanNow): $5.00 per delivery (waived for Full Suite guests).
• À La Carte Upgrade Items: Premium specialty items (such as Maine lobster tail, whole pizzas, or sushi platters) carry a flat à la carte charge across all fare tiers (Standard, Plus, and Premier), plus an 18% service charge on Standard fares.

4. Ship-Specific OceanNow® Delivery Nuances

While the core 24/7 menu above is identical across all three vessels, each ship adds subtle venue-sourced touches to its OceanNow® app catalog:
• Sun Princess (Sphere Class):
	• Expanded Beverage & Coffee Routing: Orders fulfill from multiple midship and aft service galleys across Decks 7, 9, and 17, allowing fast poolside delivery to The Dome, SeaView Terrace, and Wake View Terrace.
	• Retail & Sundries Delivery: Guests can order forgotten travel items (sunscreen, seasickness bands, charging cables, playing cards, and Princess plush toys) directly via OceanNow® for delivery to any deck chair or stateroom.
• Sky Princess (Royal Class):
	• Slice & Salty Dog Grill Integration: Daytime OceanNow® orders on Lido Decks 16 and 17 draw directly from Slice Pizzeria and Salty Dog Grill, offering rapid poolside delivery of street tacos, gourmet burgers, and loaded fries.
• Sapphire Princess (Grand Class):
	• Classic Galley & Promenade Routing: Because Sapphire features a more intimate guest count (~2,670 passengers vs. 4,300 on Sun), stateroom and Promenade Deck (Deck 7) lounge deliveries typically arrive in 15 to 25 minutes, with full bar service routed from Crooners, Explorers, and Calypso Bar.
