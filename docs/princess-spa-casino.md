Because Sun Princess (Sphere Class), Sky Princess (Royal Class), and Sapphire Princess (Grand Class) represent three distinct eras of ship architecture, their wellness, thermal suite, casino, and guest-tech layouts differ significantly. Sun Princess features a two-deck Lotus Spa with an expanded Enclave thermal suite and the largest casino in Princess history, while Sky and Sapphire Princess offer traditional forward/aft wellness sanctuaries and classic casino layouts.
Under maritime regulations, Princess Casinos operate strictly in international waters (closing in port and reopening 30–45 minutes after sailaway), whereas Lotus Spa, Fitness Centers, and OceanFront / MedallionNet Desks operate daily on both port and sea days.

1. Spa & Wellness Venues

• Lotus Spa & Salon (Sun: Decks 5 & 6 Forward | Sky: Deck 5 Forward | Sapphire: Deck 15 Forward)
	• Short Description: Full-service luxury spa and beauty salon offering massages, facials, acupuncture, medi-spa treatments, and hair/nail services.
	• Long Description: Operated in partnership with OneSpaWorld, the Lotus Spa serves as the ship’s primary holistic wellness sanctuary. Treatment rooms offer bamboo and hot-stone massages, ELEMIS Pro-Collagen facials, seaweed wraps, acupuncture, and physician-administered medi-spa services (Botox, Restylane, CoolSculpting). The adjacent salon features hair styling, keratin treatments, barber shaves, manicures, pedicures, and teeth whitening. On Sun Princess, the spa spans two full decks (Decks 5 and 6) with dedicated salt and crystal therapy rooms.
	• Hours of Operation: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily (Sea Days & Port Days; embarkation day tours run 11:30 AM – 4:30 PM).
• The Enclave Thermal Suite (Sun: Deck 5 Forward | Sky: Deck 5 Forward | Sapphire: Not Available — Features Traditional Thermal Suite on Deck 15)
	• Short Description: An exclusive, paid-access hydrotherapy and thermal sanctuary featuring heated stone loungers, specialty saunas, and a hydro-pool.
	• Long Description: Available via a week-long thermal pass (~$149–$199/person) or single-day pass on select port days, The Enclave on Sun and Sky Princess houses a warm hydrotherapy pool with cascading rain showers and air jets, heated ergonomic ceramic loungers, a Turkish hammam (steam bath), an aromatic herbal steam room, and a dry Finnish sauna. Sun Princess expands this with a dedicated Himalayan Salt Room and sensory experience showers. (Note: Sapphire Princess does not have The Enclave; instead, it offers a smaller, classic Lotus Spa Thermal Suite on Deck 15 with heated tile beds, aroma steam rooms, and complimentary dry saunas/steam rooms inside the locker rooms).
	• Hours of Operation: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily.
• Lotus Spa Fitness Center & Xponential Studio (Sun: Deck 6 Forward | Sky: Deck 17 Aft | Sapphire: Deck 15 Forward)
	• Short Description: Ocean-view cardio and weight-training gymnasium paired with a dedicated group-fitness studio for spin, yoga, and barre.
	• Long Description: Outfitted with Life Fitness and Technogym treadmills, ellipticals, Peloton-style spin bikes, free weights, and TRX suspension rigs overlooking the ocean. In partnership with Xponential Fitness, certified instructors lead daily classes inspired by Club Pilates, Pure Barre, CycleBar, YogaSix, and StretchLab (select specialty classes carry a ~$20–$25 fee unless covered by select wellness promos, while morning stretch and abs classes are complimentary).
	• Hours of Operation: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily (staffed 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM).
• The Sanctuary / Sanctuary Club (Sun: Deck 18 Forward | Sky: Deck 17 Forward | Sapphire: Deck 16 Forward)
	• Short Description: An adults-only (18+) open-air sun deck sanctuary featuring plush loungers, private cabanas, and dedicated steward service.
	• Long Description: On Sky and Sapphire Princess, The Sanctuary is a tranquil, pay-per-half-day ($20) or full-day ($40) adults-only retreat with thick-cushioned teak loungers, chilled face towels, evian misters, light healthy fare, and outdoor massage cabanas. On Sun Princess, this space is reimagined as the Sanctuary Club (Deck 18), reserved exclusively for Sanctuary Collection suite and stateroom guests with an infinity pool, private bar, and live DJ chill sessions.
	• Hours of Operation: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily (weather permitting).
