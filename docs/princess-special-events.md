To make your Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess onboard catalog truly exhaustive, there are several niche, behind-the-scenes, enrichment, and special-interest events that appear in the daily Princess Patter / MedallionClass app but fall outside standard meal periods and mainstage shows.
Below is a structured breakdown of the remaining onboard events, culinary experiences, retail tie-ins, and wellness sessions worth documenting for a complete traveler database.

1. Dining & Culinary Events (Beyond Standard Meals)

• Winemaker’s Dinner (Select Sea Days — Main Dining Room Private Alcove):
	• Details: An intimate, multi-course gourmet pairing dinner hosted by the ship’s Head Sommelier and Executive Chef for 10–12 guests (~$40–$65 per person).
	• Ships: Sun, Sky, and Sapphire Princess.
• Caymus Vineyards Winemaker Dinner (Crown Grill — Select Itineraries):
	• Details: A special five-course steakhouse tasting menu paired with prestigious Napa Valley wines from Caymus Vineyards (~$140–$160 per person).
	• Ships: Sun, Sky, and Sapphire Princess.
• 360: An Extraordinary Experience (Sun Princess / Select Ships — Exclusive):
	• Details: A 7-course, high-tech sensory dining theater combining 360-degree LED wall projections, spatial audio, synchronized table animations, and Mediterranean cuisine ($149 per person; free to select Suite guests).
• Super Tuscan / Champagne & Caviar Tastings (Vines on Sky & Sapphire | Bellini’s on Sun):
	• Details: Afternoon sommelier-led guided tastings (~$25–$45 per person) featuring prestige cuvées, Italian Super Tuscans, or whiskey/bourbon flights in Wheelhouse Bar / O’Malley’s.
• Pub Lunch (Sea Days — O’Malley’s on Sun/Sky | Crown Grill / Wheelhouse on Sapphire):
	• Details: A beloved British-style sea-day lunch pop-up serving traditional Fish & Chips, Bangers & Mash, Ploughman’s Lunch, and Bread & Butter Pudding (complimentary on Sapphire pop-up days; covered under casual dining on Sun/Sky).
• Galley Tour & Culinary Demonstration (Sea Day Morning — Princess Theater / Arena):
	• Details: A comedic, interactive cooking show hosted by the Executive Chef and Maître D’ on stage, followed by an escorted walk-through of the ship’s main galley and pastry kitchen.

2. Enrichment, Cultural & Behind-the-Scenes Events

• Ultimate Ship Tour (UST) (Final Sea Day — Ticketed Behind-the-Scenes):
	• Details: A 3-hour, small-group VIP tour (~$150 per person) granting rare access to the Navigation Bridge, Engine Control Room, Main Galley, Print Shop, Mooring Deck, and Theater Backstage, including a plush robe and chef’s jacket.
• Discovery at Sea™ & Destination Enrichment Lectures (Morning/Afternoon — Theater / Princess Live!):
	• Details: Itinerary-specific talks by historians, marine biologists, and naturalists (e.g., Glacier Calving & Humpback Whales in Alaska, Northern Lights & Viking History in Norway, or Ancient Pompeii in the Mediterranean).
• Stargazing at Sea (Weather-Permitting Nights — Top Aft Decks / SkyDeck):
	• Details: Guided night-sky constellation identification led by onboard Discovery™ specialists on dark upper decks away from ship lighting.
• Port Shopping & Ashore Talks (Sea Days — Theater / Princess Live!):
	• Details: Live presentations by the Port Shopping Ambassador highlighting recommended local merchants, gemstone buying tips, and map handouts for upcoming ports.
• Captain’s Circle Past-Passenger Party & Most Traveled Guest Luncheon:
	• Details: Invitation-only events for Ruby, Platinum, and Elite loyalty members featuring complimentary cocktails, officer introductions, and a formal luncheon honoring the top 40 most-traveled passengers onboard.
