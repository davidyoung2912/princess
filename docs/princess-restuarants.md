The three ships span three different Princess Cruises ship classes, meaning their dining layouts and restaurant lineups vary considerably. Below is a complete research guide and sample 7-day culinary rotation across the Sun Princess (Sphere Class), Sky Princess (Royal Class), and Sapphire Princess (Grand Class).

Ship-by-Ship Dining Venue Directory

Each ship organizes dining into Complimentary (Included), Casual Dining (a la carte or fixed ~$14.99 cover; included in Plus/Premier packages), and Specialty Dining ($45–$149 cover; unlimited in Premier).

1. Sun Princess (Sphere Class)

The newest flagship features a three-story main dining complex and several exclusive concepts.
• Complimentary Dining:
	• Soleil (Deck 6) & Eclipse (Deck 7): Main Dining Rooms sharing a rotating menu (traditional fixed seating in Soleil; flexible/walk-in in Eclipse).
	• The Sanctuary Restaurant (Deck 8): Exclusive private dining room for Sanctuary Collection guests.
	• The Eatery (Deck 9): Staff-served international buffet with action stations (Il Pastio pasta, Red Ginger noodles, Tex-Mex, Poke).
	• Americana Diner (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11vxstxc6_) (Deck 9): Retro-style diner serving all-day breakfast, lunch, and classic American comfort dinners.
	• International Café (Deck 9): 24-hour Piazza hub for espresso, pastries, paninis, and salads.
	• The Promenade & Lido Eats (Decks 9 & 17): Outdoor quick-service spots including Promenade Pizzeria, Lido Slice, Lido Tacos, Lido Grill, and Lido Greens.
	• Coffee & Cones / Coffee Currents: Soft-serve ice cream and specialty coffees.
• Casual Dining:
	• Alfredo’s Pizzeria (Deck 6): Sit-down Neapolitan brick-oven pizza.
	• O’Malley’s Irish Pub (Deck 7): Traditional Irish pub fare, burgers, and live music.
• Specialty & Experiential Dining:
	• Crown Grill (Deck 7): Classic steakhouse.
	• Butcher’s Block by Dario (Deck 7): Family-style Tuscan butcher experience by Dario Cecchini.
	• Sabatini’s Italian Trattoria (Deck 8): Handmade pasta and regional Italian dishes.
	• The Catch by Rudi (Deck 8): Contemporary seafood by Chef Rudi Sodamin.
	• Umai Teppanyaki & Hot Pot (Deck 8): Hibachi grill and interactive hot pot tables.
	• Makoto Ocean (Deck 8): Edomae-style sushi by Chef Makoto Okuwa.
	• Love by Britto (Deck 17): Romantic, art-inspired tasting menu designed with Romero Britto.
	• Spellbound by Magic Castle (Deck 8): Dinner and Victorian magic parlor experience.

2. Sky Princess (Royal Class)

Sky Princess balances classic Princess venues with modern casual eateries.

• Casual Dining:
	• Alfredo’s / Gigi’s Pizzeria (Deck 7): Sit-down personal Neapolitan pizzas and antipasti.
	• O’Malley’s Irish Pub (Deck 5): Pub classics, burgers, and whiskey flights.
	• Ocean Terrace Sushi Bar (Deck 7): Nigiri, sashimi, and specialty rolls overlooking the Piazza.
	• Vines Wine Bar (Deck 5): Tapas, charcuterie, and fine wines.
• Specialty & Experiential Dining:
	• Crown Grill (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F1hd_7btq3) (Deck 7): Steakhouse and seafood.

3. 7-Day Value Breakdown: Which Tier Fits Best?

For a 7-day cruise on Sky or Sapphire Princess (add $35/person total for Sun Princess):
• Princess Standard ($0 upfront + pay-as-you-go):
	• Best for: Light or non-drinkers who prefer the Main Dining Room and buffet, don't need daily Wi-Fi, and plan to eat at 0–1 specialty restaurants.
	• Baseline onboard costs: $126–$133/person in mandatory daily crew appreciation + $175 if buying 1-device Wi-Fi = ~$305/person before buying a single drink or specialty meal.
• Princess Plus ($490/person on Sky/Sapphire; $525 on Sun):
	• Best for: Guests who want Wi-Fi, gratuities covered, 2+ alcoholic/specialty coffee drinks per day, and want to try 4 casual sit-down meals (like Alfredo's or O'Malley's) plus free OceanNow delivery.
	• Break-even point: Because crew appreciation ($18/day) + Wi-Fi ($25/day) already equal $43/day, you break even as soon as you order two cocktails or specialty coffees per day.
• Princess Premier ($735/person on Sky/Sapphire; $770 on Sun):
	• Best for: Foodies who want to eat at Specialty Restaurants (Crown Grill, Sabatini's, Makoto Ocean, Catch by Rudi) almost every night, drink top-shelf cocktails/wines ($16–$20 range), use multiple Wi-Fi devices, and book a Princess shore excursion (using the $100 credit).
	• Break-even point: Compared to Princess Plus (a $245/week difference), Premier pays for itself if you eat 3 to 4 specialty dinners during the week ($165–$220 value) and use the $100 shore excursion credit.
