For a cruise booking app, cabin selection advice is one of the highest-value features you can include because two cabins in the exact same price tier can have drastically different noise levels, balcony sizes, or views.
Below is actionable, ship-specific cabin placement and category advice for Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess, structured so you can surface them as "Smart Booking Badges" or "Cabin Warnings" in your UI.

1. Sun Princess — Cabin Placement & Category Advice


Best Picks ("Sweet Spots")

• Best Quiet Midship Zone (Decks 10–12): Buffered completely by stateroom decks above and below, midship cabins on Decks 10–12 minimize ocean motion and avoid late-night venue noise while keeping you equidistant from Deck 6–9 dining and Deck 17 pools.
• Best Value Upgrade — Cabana Mini-Suites (Deck 9): Instead of a standard balcony, Cabana Mini-Suites feature an extra private indoor/outdoor lounge room ("cabana") between the bedroom and balcony, plus exclusive keycard access to the private Cabana Deck sun terrace—often priced far below full Sanctuary Suites.
• Best Budget Oceanview — Cove Balconies (Decks 6–7 Forward/Aft): Built into the ship's steel hull closer to the waterline, Cove Balconies offer dramatic up-close ocean views and extra wind/weather protection on rough sea days.
• Sanctuary Collection Sweet Spot: If booking a Sanctuary Balcony, Mini-Suite, or Suite, your fare automatically includes the Princess Premier package, private Sanctuary Restaurant dining, and access to the top-deck Sanctuary Club—making it high value if you already planned to buy Princess Premier.

Cabins to Avoid / Booking Warnings

• Deck 16 Below the Lido Deck: Cabins on Deck 16 sit directly under the main pool deck and Americana Diner (Deck 17)—expect early-morning deck chair scraping and foot traffic.
• Deck 6 Forward/Midship Above Deck 5/6 Venues & Deck 8 Below Casino/Bars: Cabins directly below O'Malley's Irish Pub, Princess Live!, or Crooners (Decks 7–8) can pick up bass and live music until midnight.
• Deck 9 Near The Eatery & Promenade: Cabins adjacent to the central elevator lobby on Deck 9 sit right next to the entrance of The Eatery food hall and outdoor Promenade grill, resulting in heavy hallway foot traffic.
• Lifeboat Obstructions on Deck 7: Verify exact cabin numbers on Deck 7—several balconies sit directly above or adjacent to lifeboat davits.

2. Sky Princess — Cabin Placement & Category Advice


Best Picks ("Sweet Spots")

• Best Quiet Midship Zone (Decks 9–14): Completely insulated from public entertainment decks (Decks 5–7) and pool decks (Deck 16).
• Always Upgrade from Standard to Deluxe Balcony: On Sky Princess, Standard Balconies (~214 sq ft) do not include a sofa or loveseat—only a single desk chair. Upgrading to a Deluxe Balcony (~233–279 sq ft) adds a comfortable indoor loveseat/sofa for minimal extra cost.
• Oversized Aft-Facing Balconies (Decks 9–14): Aft-facing cabins overlooking the wake feature significantly deeper balconies than standard side-facing cabins (which have compact ~4-foot-deep balconies).
• Best Budget Suite — Window Suites (Deck 6 Forward): Located near the Piazza/Casino level, Window Suites offer full suite square footage (~440+ sq ft) and full Suite/Reserve Collection perks without a balcony, often pricing close to a Mini-Suite.

Cabins to Avoid / Booking Warnings

• Deck 15 Under the Lido Deck: Avoid cabins directly beneath World Fresh Marketplace (buffet) and the main pools on Deck 16—early-morning crew setup and rolling carts are a common complaint.
• Deck 8 Forward & Aft (Noise + Obstructions): Deck 8 sits directly over the Princess Theater (forward) and Vista Lounge (aft), and several Deck 8 balconies have partially obstructed downward views due to lifeboats hung along Deck 7.
• No Central Stairs Above Deck 7: Warn users who book midship cabins on Decks 8–17 that there is no central passenger staircase—they will rely exclusively on the midship elevators unless they walk to the forward or aft stairs.
• Almost Zero Oceanview Cabins: Sky Princess only has 6 Deluxe Oceanview cabins on the entire ship (all at the very forward bow)—users wanting natural light without a balcony will almost always have to book a Balcony cabin.

3. Sapphire Princess — Cabin Placement & Category Advice


Best Picks ("Sweet Spots")

• The Legendary Caribe Deck (Deck 10) Balconies: Standard Balcony cabins on Caribe Deck (Deck 10) are the best-kept secret on Grand-class ships—their balconies measure roughly 9×10 feet (double the depth of balconies on Decks 9, 11, and 12) and are half-covered by Baja Deck above and half-open to the sky, giving passengers both shade/rain protection (crucial for Alaska!) and open sunbathing space.
• Best Motion-Sickness Zone (Decks 8–10 Midship): Because Sapphire Princess frequently sails rougher waters (Alaska, North Pacific, Japan, South America), midship cabins on Emerald (Deck 8) or Caribe (Deck 10) sit closest to the ship's center of gravity for the smoothest ride.
• Best Multi-Generational Setup — Two-Bedroom Family Suites (Deck 9 Forward): Two rare family suites (D105 & D106) connect a Mini-Suite and an Interior cabin via a shared living room with two bathrooms and a large balcony, sleeping up to 6–8 guests.
• Best Value Mini-Suites — Emerald Deck (Deck 8 Aft): Covered Mini-Suites at the back of Emerald Deck (like E728–E731) feature extra-wide balconies and full overhead cover from rain/wind, unlike Dolphin Deck (Deck 9) Mini-Suites whose balconies are completely uncovered and visible from upper decks.

Cabins to Avoid / Booking Warnings

• Emerald Deck (Deck 8) Obstructed Oceanviews: Dozens of Oceanview cabins on Deck 8 sit directly behind large orange lifeboats or tender vessels—some have 80–100% blocked views. Always display an "Obstructed View" warning badge for Deck 8 Oceanviews.
• Dolphin Deck (Deck 9) Exposed Mini-Suite Balconies: Almost all Mini-Suite balconies on Deck 9 protrude out from the hull with zero overhead roof, meaning passengers on Decks 10, 11, and 12 can look straight down onto your balcony, and there is no rain cover.
• Aft Cabins Directly Below Club Fusion (Deck 8 Aft): Cabins under the dance floor/stage of Club Fusion (Deck 7 aft) can hear late-night bass and karaoke.
• Forward Cabins Near the Navigation Bridge (Decks 10–12 Bow): Require blackout curtains to be closed at night and balcony lights kept off so light doesn't impair bridge officers' night vision.
• Shower Curtains in Standard Cabins: Flag for users that Interior, Oceanview, and standard Balcony cabins on Sapphire Princess have compact showers with clingy curtains rather than glass doors (Mini-Suites and above have tub/shower combos).

How Princess Cabin Numbering Works (Useful App Logic)

Across all three ships, Princess uses a predictable numbering system you can parse or explain to users:
• Deck Prefix:
	• Sun Princess: Uses numeric deck prefixes (5xxx = Deck 5, 10xxx = Deck 10, 16xxx = Deck 16).
	• Sky & Sapphire Princess: Use letter prefixes matching traditional deck names:
		• P = Plaza (Deck 5)
		• E = Emerald (Deck 8)
		• D = Dolphin (Deck 9)
		• C = Caribe (Deck 10)
		• B = Baja (Deck 11)
		• A = Aloha (Deck 12)
		• R = Riviera (Deck 14 — Sky Princess) / L = Lido (Deck 14 — Sapphire Princess)
		• M = Marina (Deck 15 — Sky Princess)
		• L = Lido (Deck 16 — Sky Princess)
		• S = Sun (Deck 17 — Sky Princess)
• Port vs. Starboard: Odd-numbered cabins are on the Starboard (right) side; Even-numbered cabins are on the Port (left) side.
• Fore-to-Aft Progression: Numbers start in the 100s at the forward bow and increase toward the 700s at the aft stern.

1. Sun Princess — Exact Deck Distribution & Cabin Inventory

Sun Princess houses 2,157–2,162 cabins across 11 decks (Decks 5–6 and 9–17; Decks 4, 7, 8, and 18–21 contain zero staterooms).

Deck-by-Deck Cabin Breakdown

• Deck 5 (161 cabins): 134 Premium Oceanview (forward/midship), 27 Interior. (Note: Forward-most oceanviews are near the Lotus Spa).
• Deck 6 (158 cabins): 100 Cove Balconies (midship, built into the steel hull close to the waterline), 40 Premium Oceanview (forward), 18 Interior.
• Deck 9 (116 cabins): 78 Cabana Mini-Suites (lining the private Cabana Deck promenade), 4 Deluxe Balconies, 34 Interior.
• Deck 10 (281 cabins): 112 Suites & Mini-Suites (including Cabana Mini-Suites overlooking the Cabana Deck below), 108 Deluxe Balconies, 6 Forward Oceanviews, 55 Interior.
• Deck 11 (294 cabins — Largest cabin deck): 59 Suites & Mini-Suites (including forward/aft Signature Suites & Cabana Mini-Suites), 164 Deluxe Balconies, 6 Forward Oceanviews, 65 Interior.
• Deck 12 (276 cabins): 58 Suites & Mini-Suites, 158 Deluxe Balconies, 60 Interior.
• Deck 14 (271 cabins): 52 Suites & Mini-Suites, 160 Deluxe Balconies, 59 Interior.
• Deck 15 (256 cabins): 40 Suites & Mini-Suites, 146 Deluxe Balconies, 70 Interior. (Note: Navigation Bridge is forward on Deck 15).
• Deck 16 (248 cabins): 36 Suites (including flagship Signature Sky & Owners Suites aft), 154 Deluxe Balconies, 58 Interior.
• Deck 17 (96 cabins): 34 Suites & Mini-Suites (forward/midship), 48 Deluxe Balconies, 14 Interior.

Notable Cabin Codes & Exact Room Numbers

• 2-Bedroom Signature Sky Suites (Category S1 — 4 cabins): Located aft on Deck 16 (e.g., 16617) with 1,118–1,262 sq ft and wrap-around balconies.
• Signature Owners Suites (Category S2 — 10 cabins): Located on Decks 10 & 16 (~564–565 sq ft).
• Signature Penthouse Suites (Category S3 — 36 cabins): Located on Decks 15–17 (~305–635 sq ft).
• Sanctuary Collection Categories: Category codes DO (Sanctuary Deluxe Balcony), MD (Sanctuary Mini-Suite), and S1–S4 include automatic Princess Premier perks, private Sanctuary Restaurant dining, and Sanctuary Club access.
• Bed-Near-Balcony vs. Sofa-Near-Balcony: On Sun Princess, Deluxe Balconies alternate layouts every other cabin—some place the bed closer to the balcony glass, while adjacent rooms place the sofa closer to the balcony (a frequently searched detail on cruise forums).

2. Sky Princess — Exact Deck Distribution & Cabin Inventory

Sky Princess houses 1,830 cabins across 10 decks (Decks 8–12 and 14–18).

Deck-by-Deck Cabin Breakdown

• Deck 8 (Emerald): Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors. (Contains multiple obstructed-view balconies midship/aft due to lifeboats hung at Deck 7).
• Deck 9 (Dolphin): Vista Suites (aft corners), Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors.
• Deck 10 (Caribe): Penthouse Suites (aft), Vista Suites (aft corners), Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors.
• Deck 11 (Baja): Penthouse Suites, Vista Suites, Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors.
• Deck 12 (Aloha): Penthouse Suites, Vista Suites, Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors.
• Deck 14 (Riviera): Penthouse Suites (aft), Owners Suites (e.g., R617 midship), Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, Interiors, plus the Suite Concierge Lounge. (Bridge is forward).
• Deck 15 (Marina): Penthouse Suites, Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors. (Sits directly below Lido pools/buffet).
• Deck 16 (Lido): Forward cabins only (ahead of the Lido pools)—Mini-Suites, Deluxe Balconies, Standard Balconies, and Interiors.
• Deck 17 (Sun): Forward cabins only (ahead of The Sanctuary and Retreat Pool)—including the ship's only 6 Deluxe Oceanview cabins (slanted floor-to-ceiling bow windows) plus forward Balconies/Suites.
• Deck 18 (Sky): Houses only 2 staterooms—the two flagship Sky Suites (S101 & S102 / forward-midship overlooking the Lido screen).

Notable Cabin Codes & Exact Room Numbers

• Sky Suites (Category S1 — 2 cabins): Located on Deck 18 (~1,012–1,260 sq ft including a ~700 sq ft wrap-around veranda).
• Owners Suites (Category S4): Located midship on Deck 14 (Riviera) (e.g., R617 — 440 sq ft interior + 73 sq ft balcony).
• Penthouse & Vista Suites (Categories S2, S3, S5): Located primarily at the aft stern corners across Decks 8–15, offering deep wrap-around wake balconies.
• Metal vs. Glass Balcony Railings: Forward-most balconies on Decks 8–16 (Categories BF and DW) have solid steel-fronted railings rather than clear glass due to wind/spray at the bow.
• May 2027 Sanctuary Collection Rollout: Starting May 2027, select Suites, Mini-Suites, and Deluxe Balconies on Sky Princess convert into the all-inclusive Sanctuary Collection tier.

3. Sapphire Princess — Exact Deck Distribution & Cabin Inventory

Sapphire Princess houses 1,337–1,339 cabins across 7 decks (Decks 5, 8–12, and 14).

Deck-by-Deck Cabin Breakdown

• Deck 5 (Plaza): ~30 Oceanview cabins and ~20 Interior cabins tucked forward/midship near The Piazza and Lotus Spa. (Very low motion in rough seas).
• Deck 8 (Emerald): Mostly Obstructed & Unobstructed Oceanview cabins (forward/midship behind lifeboats), Interiors, and a small cluster of covered Mini-Suites at the far aft stern (E728–E731).
• Deck 9 (Dolphin): Dedicated almost entirely to Mini-Suites (with uncovered, open-air balconies protruding from the hull), Interiors, and 2 Two-Bedroom Family Suites forward (D105 & D106).
• Deck 10 (Caribe): Home to the famous extra-deep half-covered/half-open Balcony cabins (~9×10 ft balconies), Aft Suites, and Interiors.
• Deck 11 (Baja): Standard Balcony cabins (fully covered, ~9×5 ft balconies), Aft Suites, and Interiors.
• Deck 12 (Aloha): Standard Balcony cabins (fully covered), Aft Suites (including the flagship Grand Suite), and Interiors.
• Deck 14 (Lido): ~40 forward Balcony and Interior cabins located ahead of the Conservatory Pool and Neptune’s Reef Pool.

Notable Cabin Codes & Exact Room Numbers

• Grand Suite (Category S1 — 1 cabin): Located on Deck 12 (Aloha) aft (A748 / ~1,314 sq ft total), featuring a living room with fireplace, dining table, wet bar, whirlpool bath, and huge wake balcony.
• Two-Bedroom Family Suites (Category S8 — 2 cabins): Located forward on Deck 9 (Dolphin)—specifically D105 (connects to D101) and D106 (connects to D102), sleeping up to 8 guests across two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a shared living room, and a large balcony.
• Covered Aft Mini-Suites (E728–E731): Located at the back of Deck 8 (Emerald)—unlike Dolphin Deck Mini-Suites, these have full ceiling cover and extra-wide balconies.
• Aft Wake Suites (C748–C753, B748–B753, D736–D741): Owners, Penthouse, and Vista Suites stacked along the stern on Decks 9–12, offering panoramic wake views.
