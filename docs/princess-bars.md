The Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess feature a mix of classic fleetwide piano lounges, Irish pubs, bespoke cocktail laboratories, and ship-exclusive speakeasies.
Under Princess Cruises' beverage program, Princess Plus covers drinks priced up to $15 USD (15 alcoholic drinks/day, unlimited zero-proof mocktails, specialty coffees, and sodas), while Princess Premier covers unlimited drinks up to $20 USD (with a 25% discount on bottles of wine). For any drink exceeding your package cap, you pay only the price difference plus an 18% service charge on that difference.
