Because Sun Princess (Sphere Class), Sky Princess (Royal Class), and Sapphire Princess (Grand Class) represent three distinct generations of Princess ship design, their retail footprints range from a massive, two-deck luxury flagship promenade on Sun Princess to traditional duty-free atrium boutiques on Sky and Sapphire Princess.
Under international maritime customs regulations, all onboard retail shops remain closed while the ship is docked in port and open approximately 30 to 45 minutes after sailing into international waters.

1. Standard Operating Hours & Maritime Retail Rules

• Sea Days: 9:30 AM – 10:00 PM (continuous operation across all boutiques).
• Port Days: Closed while alongside the pier; opens 30–45 minutes after sailaway until 10:00 PM or 11:00 PM.
• Embarkation Day (Day 1): Opens 45 minutes after departure (typically 5:30 PM – 10:30 PM).
• Photo Gallery & Fine Arts Gallery: Displays remain open 24/7 for browsing; staffed desks operate 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM on sea days and 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM on port days.
• Tax & Duty-Free Advantage: All onboard purchases are free of local sales tax and import duties while in international waters.

2. Ship-by-Ship Permanent Shopping Venue Directory


A. Sun Princess (Sphere Class — Decks 7 & 8)

Sun Princess features the largest retail footprint in Princess history—spanning two full decks around The Piazza with over 200 premium brands and multiple "first-at-sea" standalone boutiques.
• The Shops of Princess — Flagship Logo & Souvenir Store (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: The central hub for Princess Cruises branded apparel, ship models, destination souvenirs, and travel essentials.
	• Long Description: Located along the bustling Deck 7 Piazza promenade, this expansive store stocks exclusive Sun Princess inaugural and Sphere-class merchandise, collectible die-cast ship models, Stanley the Bear plush toys, destination-specific hoodies and caps, and forgotten travel sundries (sunscreen, over-the-counter medications, adapters, and snacks).
• The Beauty & Fragrance Studio (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A modern, open-concept cosmetics and perfumery hall featuring luxury skincare, makeup, and signature scents.
	• Long Description: Styled like a contemporary luxury department store beauty floor, this boutique offers tax- and duty-free counters for Chanel, Dior, Lancôme, Estée Lauder, Clinique, and Jo Malone, complete with complimentary mini-makeovers, skincare consultations, and fragrance profiling.
• Fine Timepieces & Luxury Horology Salon (Deck 8 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A dedicated luxury watch gallery showcasing Swiss horology and certified pre-owned investment pieces.
	• Long Description: Positioned on the upscale Deck 8 gallery overlooking the Piazza, this salon features dedicated brand corners for TAG Heuer, Breitling, Longines, Tissot, Rado, and Hamilton, alongside a curated vault of certified pre-owned Rolex and Cartier timepieces.
• Effy Fine Jewelry Boutique (Deck 8 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A flagship fine jewelry salon specializing in rare colored gemstones, diamonds, and exclusive Princess collections.
	• Long Description: Anchoring the luxury jewelry wing on Deck 8, Effy offers private viewing salons, custom ring settings, tanzanite and emerald suites, and complimentary daily charm-bracelet giveaways during scheduled promotional hours.
• Luxury Fashion & Accessories Gallery (Deck 8 — Midship)
	• Short Description: Curated designer handbags, resort wear, eyewear, and Italian leather goods.
	• Long Description: Featuring dedicated shop-in-shop boutiques—including a Lacoste "Le Club" concept store, Swarovski crystal gallery, Ray-Ban & Maui Jim sunglass bar, and certified pre-owned luxury handbags (Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Chanel)—tailored for resort and formal-night styling.
• Spirits, Wine & Tobacco Duty-Free Market (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A sleek liquor and confectionery emporium offering rare single-malt scotches, small-batch bourbons, and Swiss chocolates.
	• Long Description: Stocked with travel-exclusive liter bottles of premium spirits, vintage champagnes, regional wines, and luxury chocolates (Godiva, Toblerone, Lindt). Note that alcohol purchased for take-home consumption is held by the ship and delivered to your stateroom on the final evening.
• Fine Arts Gallery & Photo Studio (Deck 7 — Forward/Midship)
	• Short Description: Interactive digital photo kiosks and a curated art gallery featuring works by Romero Britto, Thomas Kinkade, and Peter Max.
	• Long Description: Combines facial-recognition digital photo stations (linked to your OceanMedallion) with a walk-through fine art exhibition showcasing framed lithographs, sculptures, and original paintings available for direct purchase or live auction.

B. Sky Princess (Royal Class — Decks 5, 6 & 7)

Sky Princess arranges its boutiques in an elegant, classic horseshoe around the three-story marble Piazza.
• Calypso Cove (Deck 5 — Piazza Level)
	• Short Description: Convenience, duty-free liquor, tobacco, snacks, and Princess logo souvenir shop.
	• Long Description: Conveniently positioned right off the Deck 5 Piazza floor, Calypso Cove is the go-to stop for duty-free spirits, travel-size toiletries, over-the-counter remedies, chocolates, and casual Princess Cruises T-shirts and magnets.
• Essence (Deck 6 — Piazza Level)
	• Short Description: Dedicated duty-free fragrance, skincare, and luxury cosmetics boutique.
	• Long Description: Showcasing top international beauty houses including Dior, Chanel, Guerlain, Clinique, and Estée Lauder at up to 25–30% off U.S. mainland retail prices, with beauty specialists offering complimentary skin diagnostics.
• Facets (Deck 6 — Piazza Level)
	• Short Description: High-end fine jewelry and Swiss watch boutique featuring Effy, Tissot, and Longines.
	• Long Description: A bright, glass-fronted salon dedicated to diamond solitaires, colored gemstones, and Swiss mechanical and quartz watches, hosting nightly trunk shows and gemstone seminars.
• Meridian Bay (Deck 7 — Promenade Level)
	• Short Description: Fashion jewelry, designer sunglasses, handbags, and eveningwear accessories boutique.
	• Long Description: Located along the upper Piazza gallery on Deck 7, Meridian Bay stocks Swarovski crystal jewelry, Brighton handbags and charms, designer sunglasses, pashmina wraps, and formal evening clutches.
• Limelight / Fashion Boutique (Deck 7 — Promenade Level)
	• Short Description: Resort apparel, smart-casual cruise wear, and destination-themed outerwear.
	• Long Description: Carries easy-to-pack resort wear, breathable linen shirts, swimwear, and—on Northern European or Alaska sailings—weatherproof jackets, fleece pullovers, and cashmere scarves.
• Photo & Fine Arts Galleries (Decks 6 & 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: Portrait studios, digital photo galleries, and Park West fine art exhibitions.

C. Sapphire Princess (Grand Class — Decks 6 & 7)

Sapphire Princess features a classic, intimate Grand-Class boutique cluster flanking the upper levels of the Atrium on Fiesta (Deck 6) and Promenade (Deck 7).
• Calypso Cove (Deck 6 — Fiesta Deck)
	• Short Description: Shipboard general store for Princess logo apparel, destination gifts, snacks, and duty-free liquor.
	• Long Description: Located steps from the Princess Theater and Atrium on Deck 6, Calypso Cove offers destination souvenirs (such as Alaskan ulu knives, glacier apparel, or Japanese keepsakes depending on itinerary), duty-free spirits, and travel necessities.
• Essence (Deck 6 — Fiesta Deck)
	• Short Description: Tax- and duty-free designer perfumes, colognes, and skincare boutique.
	• Long Description: Offers travel-exclusive fragrance sets and luxury cosmetics from Lancôme, Clinique, Chanel, and Estée Lauder.
• Facets (Deck 7 — Promenade Deck)
	• Short Description: Fine jewelry and luxury timepiece salon specializing in Effy jewelry and Swiss watches.
	• Long Description: Positioned along the Deck 7 Promenade walkway, Facets hosts diamond unveiling events, tanzanite showcases, and watch consultations.
• Meridian Bay (Deck 7 — Promenade Deck)
	• Short Description: Contemporary fashion jewelry, Swarovski crystals, watches, and designer handbags.
	• Long Description: A popular browsing spot during evening promenades, offering fashion watches (Fossil, Citizen, Bulova), Brighton accessories, and sparkly evening wear.
• Limelight Boutique (Deck 7 — Promenade Deck)
	• Short Description: Destination-tailored clothing, formal accessories, and resort fashion.
	• Long Description: Stocks men’s and women’s casual wear, formal-night ties and shawls, and seasonal outdoor gear tailored to Sapphire’s Pacific, Alaska, or South America itineraries.
• Princess Fine Arts & Photo Gallery (Deck 7 — Promenade Deck)
	• Short Description: Walk-through art gallery and photo desk located adjacent to Explorers Lounge and Wheelhouse Bar.

3. Temporary Pop-Up Sales & Special Retail Events

In addition to permanent boutiques, Princess Cruises sets up pop-up retail tables and flash sales in high-traffic public areas—primarily The Piazza (Atrium floor), Poolside Lido Decks, Vista / Explorers Lounges, and Princess Live!:
• The Captain’s Table / Piazza Sidewalk Mega-Sale: Pop-up folding tables arranged across the Piazza floor featuring $10–$20 "Grab & Go" flash deals on pashmina shawls, faux-leather handbags, fashion watches, costume jewelry sets, and destination T-shirts.
• Inch of Gold / Inch of Silver Custom Chain Bar: A temporary counter set up in the Piazza or Promenade where specialists measure and custom-clasp gold- and silver-layered chains by the inch for necklaces and bracelets.
• Sip & Sale / VIP Evening Trunk Shows: Hosted in Facets, Effy, or The Piazza with complimentary sparkling wine, raffles, and limited-edition jewelry unveilings.
• Poolside Lido Deck Pop-Up Market: Weather-permitting outdoor pop-up tables set up near the main pool on sea days selling sunglasses, sun hats, cover-ups, and logo apparel.
• Champagne Art Auctions (Vista Lounge / Explorers Lounge / Princess Live!): Live, fast-paced art auctions hosted by Park West Gallery auctioneers featuring complimentary champagne, "guess the weight of the sculpture" contests, and $50–$500 "mystery art" blind bids.
• Destination Artisan & Local Pop-Ups: On select itineraries (such as Alaska, Mexico, or the Mediterranean), local artisans board the ship for one afternoon to sell authentic regional crafts (e.g., Native Alaskan carvings, Murano glass, or Peruvian alpaca goods) in the Piazza.
