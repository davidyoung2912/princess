Because the Sun Princess (Sphere Class), Sky Princess (Royal Class), and Sapphire Princess (Grand Class) represent three distinct eras of ship architecture, their entertainment venues differ significantly—from Sun Princess's futuristic geodesic Dome and in-the-round Arena to Sky's dedicated jazz theater and Sapphire's classic aft lounges and sky-high nightclub.
Below is a comprehensive, copy-ready Markdown guide detailing the entertainment venues across all three vessels—with both short and long descriptions—followed by full 7-day Morning, Noon, and Night event schedules tailored to each ship.

Princess Cruises: Comprehensive Entertainment Venue & 7-Day Schedule Guide

Covered Vessels: Sun Princess (Sphere Class) | Sky Princess (Royal Class) | Sapphire Princess (Grand Class)

Part 1: Ship-by-Ship Entertainment Venue Directory


1. Sun Princess (Sphere Class)

The flagship of Princess's next-generation Sphere Class reimagines onboard entertainment with transformable architecture and multi-deck social hubs.
• Princess Arena (Decks 6, 7 & 8 — Forward)
	• Short Description: A technologically advanced, transformable theater-in-the-round hosting headline Broadway-style production shows.
	• Long Description: Replacing the traditional proscenium cruise theater, the Princess Arena features movable seating banks and a rotating central stage that configures into three distinct layouts (in-the-round, keyhole, and traditional). Equipped with surround-sound acoustics, massive LED backdrops, and aerial rigging, it hosts flagship productions like Viva La Música, Vallora: A Pirate Quest, Stage Struck, and Fiera!, alongside headline guest comedians and vocalists.
• The Dome (Decks 17 & 18 — Forward)
	• Short Description: A multi-level glass-enclosed geodesic dome serving as an indoor/outdoor pool deck by day and an acrobatic theater by night.
	• Long Description: Inspired by the cliffside terraces of Santorini, The Dome features a sweeping glass roof, an indoor/outdoor waterfall pool, and cascading lounge seating. During the day, it offers climate-controlled relaxation with panoramic ocean views; at night, the pool covers over to form a stage for high-flying Cirque Éloize productions (Blue, Come Fly Away, ArtBeat), aerialists, and illuminated late-night DJ dance parties.
• The Piazza (Decks 7, 8 & 9 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A three-story glass-walled architectural atrium serving as the ship’s central hub for live music, street performers, and pop-up parties.
	• Long Description: Unlike older ships where the atrium is interior, Sun Princess’s Piazza is encased in a massive glass sphere looking directly out to the ocean. Anchored by a central circular stage and retractable LED screen, it buzzes from morning to midnight with string quartets, acoustic duos, classical pianists, aerial pop-up acts, dance classes, and the Captain’s Champagne Waterfall.
• Princess Live! (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: An intimate broadcast-style TV studio and lounge hosting interactive game shows, trivia, and late-night comedy.
	• Long Description: Styled like a contemporary television soundstage complete with camera rigs, theatrical lighting, and plush banquette seating, Princess Live! is the epicenter of cruise director-hosted games. By day it hosts trivia, culinary demos, and Wake Show tapings; by night it transforms into a comedy club and game show arena for Deal or No Deal, The Marriage Match, and Majority Rules.
• O’Malley’s Irish Pub (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A lively traditional Irish pub featuring nightly live folk and acoustic rock musicians alongside pub fare and whiskey flights.
	• Long Description: Finished in dark mahogany, brass accents, and cozy leather booths, O’Malley’s brings authentic Dublin pub energy to sea. Resident soloists and duos perform Celtic classics, acoustic sing-alongs, and modern pub-rock sets every evening while guests enjoy pints of Guinness and Irish whiskey tastings.
• Wheelhouse Bar (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: An upscale, maritime-themed cocktail lounge offering nightly live piano music and classic ballroom dancing.
	• Long Description: Steeped in classic nautical heritage with rich wood paneling, brass ship models, and plush armchairs, the Wheelhouse Bar provides a refined escape from the high-energy atrium. It hosts afternoon board games and quiet reading before transitioning into an elegant evening venue for resident pianists, jazz combos, and slow dancing.
• SeaView Terrace & Wake View Terrace (Decks 17 & 8 — Forward & Aft)
	• Short Description: Multi-deck outdoor pool and entertainment decks hosting sailaway parties, live deck bands, and outdoor cinema.
	• Long Description: Sun Princess splits its outdoor deck energy between the forward SeaView Terrace (featuring the Movies Under the Stars giant LED screen and main pool stage) and the multi-tiered aft Wake View Terrace (featuring an infinity pool and sunset bar). Together they host embarkation sailaway parties, poolside DJ sets, theme deck parties, and evening blockbuster screenings.
• Spellbound by Magic Castle (Deck 8 — Midship)
	• Short Description: An exclusive, Victorian-themed speakeasy parlor blending theatrical mixology with close-up sleight-of-hand magic.
	• Long Description: Created in partnership with Hollywood’s famed Magic Castle, this ticketed experiential venue hides behind an unmarked door. Guests explore secret rooms filled with mystical artifacts, sip theatrical smoking cocktails in the Music Room, and gather in a 30-seat velvet-draped theater for intimate, world-class illusionists.

2. Sky Princess (Royal Class)

Sky Princess pairs Royal-Class grandeur with specialized live music lounges and outdoor water-and-light spectacles.
• Princess Theater (Decks 6 & 7 — Forward)
	• Short Description: A grand, two-deck Broadway-style proscenium theater showcasing full-scale musicals, vocalists, and illusionists.
	• Long Description: Featuring plush tiered seating with unobstructed sightlines, a live showband orchestra pit, and state-of-the-art digital projection mapping, the Princess Theater is the premier evening showplace. It headlines Princess’s acclaimed original productions (Rock Opera, 5-SKIES, and Spotlight Bar) alongside touring headliners.
• Take Five (Deck 6 — Midship)
	• Short Description: Princess’s exclusive, immersive jazz theater featuring nightly live trios, jazz history storytelling, and craft cocktails.
	• Long Description: Styled after a mid-century Harlem jazz club with exposed brick, moody neon lighting, and vintage photography, Take Five is the only dedicated jazz theater at sea. By day it hosts Latin salsa lessons and acoustic sessions; at night, a resident jazz trio performs themed sets (A Night in Harlem, The Birth of BeBop, Afro-Cuban Jazz) paired with master mixologist cocktails.
• Vista Lounge (Deck 7 — Aft)
	• Short Description: An intimate, cabaret-style aft show lounge hosting comedians, live party bands, game shows, and late-night theme parties.
	• Long Description: Spanning the stern of Deck 7 with sweeping wrap-around windows and a central circular dance floor, Vista Lounge offers an up-close cabaret atmosphere. It serves as a versatile hub for daytime art auctions and bingo, transitioning at night into a high-energy showcase for stand-up comics, tribute bands, The Voice of the Ocean, and 70s/80s disco nights.
• Princess Live! & Princess Live! Café (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A purpose-built interactive TV studio venue hosting trivia, culinary shows, game shows, and late-night comedy.
	• Long Description: Located right off the midship thoroughfare with its own dedicated espresso and cocktail bar, Princess Live! invites guests into a studio audience environment. Equipped with audience response keypads and multi-camera feeds broadcast to cabin TVs, it hosts daily trivia tournaments, passenger talent auditions, and late-night adult comedy.
• The Piazza (Decks 5, 6 & 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A three-story Italian-inspired marble atrium featuring continuous live music, acrobatic pop-up acts, and gala parties.
	• Long Description: Surrounded by Bellini’s, Vines, Crooners, and the International Café, Sky Princess’s Piazza is an energetic European town square at sea. Throughout the day and evening, guests gather around the marble floor to watch classical string ensembles, ballroom dance demonstrations, fruit-carving demos, and the signature Champagne Waterfall.
• Top Deck Pool & Movies Under the Stars (Decks 16 & 17 — Midship)
	• Short Description: An expansive twin-pool lido deck anchored by a 300-square-foot outdoor LED cinema screen and stage.
	• Long Description: By day, the Fountain Pool and Main Pool host live Caribbean/party bands, poolside Olympics, and ice-carving demonstrations. By night, the poolside loungers are dressed with fleece blankets for complimentary popcorn and blockbuster screenings on Movies Under the Stars, interspersed with synchronized Water & Light fountain shows and deck dance parties.
• Crown Grill Bar & Crooners (Decks 7 & 6)
	• Short Description: Classic cocktail and piano lounges offering intimate acoustic sets and martini-fueled sing-alongs.
	• Long Description: Crooners (overlooking the Piazza on Deck 6) celebrates the Rat Pack era with 75+ specialty martinis and a charismatic piano entertainer taking requests late into the night. Meanwhile, Crown Grill Bar on Deck 7 offers plush leather seating and nightly acoustic guitarists or jazz duos.

3. Sapphire Princess (Grand Class)

Sapphire Princess delivers a classic, spacious Grand-Class layout celebrated for its wrap-around promenade, iconic aft nightclub, and multiple distinct live-music lounges.
• Princess Theater (Decks 6 & 7 — Forward)
	• Short Description: A classic two-tier ocean-liner theater showcasing Broadway-style production shows, classical concerts, and variety headliners.
	• Long Description: Designed with warm wood accents, plush theater seating, and an expansive main stage, the Princess Theater hosts evening production revues (Bravo, Encore, Magic to Do, or Born to Dance) featuring the Princess Singers, Dancers, and live showband, as well as afternoon enrichment lectures and classical recitals.
• Club Fusion (Deck 7 — Aft)
	• Short Description: A high-tech, country-meets-cabaret aft lounge featuring multiple video screens, a large dance floor, and interactive game shows.
	• Long Description: Spanning the aft end of the Promenade Deck, Club Fusion is outfitted with dozens of synchronized flat-screen monitors, theatrical lighting rigs, and a spacious wooden dance floor. It is the primary hub for Princess Pop Star karaoke, line-dancing lessons, bingo, live party bands, and themed dance nights.
• Explorers Lounge (Deck 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: An eclectic, globally themed midship lounge hosting trivia, art auctions, comedy acts, and live cover bands.
	• Long Description: Decorated with safari, Egyptian, and Silk Road motifs, the Explorers Lounge sits along the main Deck 7 thoroughfare with its own full bar and stage. It is continuously active from morning trivia and port talks to evening audience-participation game shows (Majority Rules, Liar’s Club), guest comedians, and live rock/pop sets.
• Wheelhouse Bar (Deck 7 — Forward)
	• Short Description: A traditional, clubby nautical lounge offering quiet daytime relaxation and lively evening ballroom and blues dancing.
	• Long Description: Featuring dark mahogany paneling, vintage maritime memorabilia, and a generous hardwood dance floor, the Wheelhouse Bar is a favorite among dancers and cocktail enthusiasts. During the day it serves as a peaceful reading and card-playing sanctuary; in the evening, live bands perform ballroom, swing, and classic blues sets.
• Skywalkers Nightclub (Deck 17 — Aft)
	• Short Description: An iconic, suspended glass-enclosed lounge perched 150 feet above the sea—a serene observation deck by day and a high-energy nightclub by night.
	• Long Description: Accessible via a dramatic glass-walled moving walkway, Skywalkers cantilevers high above the ship’s stern with 270-degree panoramic ocean views. By day, it offers quiet tiered seating for reading and scenic cruising (especially in Alaska or the Norwegian fjords); after 10:00 PM, its neon-lit dance floor pulses with resident DJ sets.
• The Piazza & Crooners Lounge (Decks 5, 6 & 7 — Midship)
	• Short Description: A three-deck European-style atrium and adjacent piano martini bar serving as the ship’s social heart.
	• Long Description: Anchored by the International Café and Vines Wine Bar on Deck 5 and flanked by Crooners piano bar on Deck 7, the Piazza hosts string quartets, ballroom dance lessons, street-style variety performers, and the Captain’s Welcome Champagne Waterfall.
• Neptune’s Reef & Pool / Movies Under the Stars (Deck 14 — Midship)
	• Short Description: The main outdoor lido pool complex featuring a giant poolside LED screen, live bandstand, and deck parties.
	• Long Description: Surrounded by tiered sun decks and hot tubs, the main pool hosts embarkation sailaway celebrations, live Caribbean/party bands, and poolside games by day, transforming into an open-air cinema with blankets and complimentary popcorn under the stars each evening.

2. Core Venue Descriptions

• Ages 3–7 — Firefly Park (Sun) / The Treehouse (Sky & Sapphire):
	• Short Description: Whimsical, forest- and glow-themed play centers designed for crafts, storytelling, dress-up, and active group games.
	• Long Description: On Sky and Sapphire, The Treehouse immerses young cruisers in a bright woodland cabin atmosphere filled with creature puppets, reading nooks, mini climbing structures, and craft tables. On Sun Princess, Firefly Park expands the concept into a magical bioluminescent forest spanning Deck 6, adding a dedicated soft-play sensory zone for babies and toddlers (ages 6 months–2 years) alongside interactive digital floor projections and puppet theaters.
• Ages 8–12 — Neon Grove (Sun) / The Lodge (Sky & Sapphire):
	• Short Description: High-energy tween hangouts blending Discovery-themed science labs, sports tournaments, and next-gen video gaming.
	• Long Description: Inspired by the great outdoors, The Lodge (Sky & Sapphire) features cozy campfire-style seating, air hockey, foosball, gaming stations, and lab tables for MythBusters experiments. On Sun Princess, Neon Grove upgrades the space into a vibrant, neon-lit clubhouse outfitted with PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch consoles, skeeball, air hockey, and plush modular lounge pods.
• Ages 13–17 — The Underground (Sun) / The Beach House (Sky & Sapphire):
	• Short Description: Adults-and-kids-free teen lounges featuring console gaming, jukeboxes, mocktail socials, and late-night dance parties.
	• Long Description: The Beach House (Sky & Sapphire) channels a laid-back California surf shack with hammocks, oversized beanbags, video game tournaments, and photo booths. The Underground on Sun Princess takes inspiration from urban street art and subway lounges, offering PS5 and Switch gaming booths, skeeball, foosball, a DJ booth, and chill-out zones.

3. Operating Hours & Key Policies

• Sea Day Hours: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM | 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM | 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Closed 12:00–1:00 PM and 5:00–6:00 PM for staff meal breaks and sanitization; Teens open 10:00 AM – 12:00 AM).
• Port Day Supervision: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM continuous complimentary supervision for registered kids whose parents are ashore (pre-registration required by 8:00 PM the night before; youth staff escort kids to the buffet for lunch).
• Late-Night Group Kidsitting (Ages 3–12): Available nightly from 10:00 PM – 1:00 AM for $5 USD per child, per hour (must sign up by 8:00 PM).
• Sign-In / Sign-Out Rules: Ages 3–7 require strict parent/guardian check-in and check-out with matching Medallion/ID. Ages 8–12 may self-sign out only with written parental permission. Teens (13–17) enjoy open come-and-go access.

The Sun Princess’s top-deck family zone (Decks 19–21)—originally marketed as Park19 and later rebranded to SkyDeck Sport Court & Track—underwent significant changes following the ship's 2024 launch.
Important Operational Update: Princess Cruises announced that the three primary thrill rides—the Sea Breeze Rollglider, Coastal Climb, and The Net ropes course—did not receive final vendor certification and were permanently closed without opening to guests. The remaining recreational amenities on Deck 19 operate daily as complimentary, open-air family and fitness spaces.

Part 1: Active & Operational Attractions (Open Daily)


1. Splash Zone (Deck 19 — Aft)

• Short Description: An open-air water play pad featuring interactive pop-jet fountains and misting spray elements for younger children.
• Long Description: Located at the aft end of Deck 19, the Splash Zone offers a shallow, zero-depth aquatic play area equipped with ground-level water jets, colorful spray nozzles, and gentle fountains. Designed primarily for toddlers and young kids to cool off in warm-weather itineraries, it sits adjacent to shaded family lounge seating and deck chairs. (Note: Operation is weather- and itinerary-dependent, typically active on warm Caribbean and Mediterranean sailings.)

2. Multi-Use Sports / Recreational Court (Deck 19 — Aft)

• Short Description: An enclosed, open-air top-deck court hosting pickleball, basketball, ping-pong, shuffleboard, and group fitness classes.
• Long Description: Surrounded by high safety netting and panoramic ocean views, this versatile recreational court serves as the hub for active family games and youth club tournaments. During the day, it rotates between open-play basketball, lined pickleball courts, table tennis, shuffleboard, and lawn games like cornhole and giant Connect Four. In the mornings, it doubles as an outdoor studio for Xponential Fitness classes, sunrise stretching, and guided meditation.

3. Open-Air Jogging Track (Deck 19 — Perimeter)

• Short Description: A dedicated, cushioned outdoor walking and running track encircling the upper aft deck (6.7 laps per mile).
• Long Description: Looping around the perimeter of Deck 19 above the main lido pools, this scenic track provides runners and walkers with unobstructed sea breezes and 360-degree ocean views. Clear distance markers allow fitness-minded guests to track mileage easily (approximately 6.7 to 7 laps equal one full mile).

4. Infinite Horizon Tilt Walls (Deck 19 — Port & Starboard Sides)

• Short Description: Angled, cantilevered glass viewing walls extending over the edge of Deck 19 for dramatic straight-down ocean views.
• Long Description: Designed for height enthusiasts and photo opportunities, Infinite Horizon consists of outward-leaning, floor-to-ceiling tempered glass panels cantilevered over the ship’s hull. Guests can lean safely against the angled glass to look 10 decks straight down toward the Promenade (Deck 9) and the wake churning below.

5. Hammock Area & Lawn Games Lounge (Deck 19 — Central)

• Short Description: A relaxed, shaded social zone outfitted with woven rope hammocks, lounge chairs, and oversized deck games.
• Long Description: Situated beside the sports court, this casual hangout features clusters of swinging hammocks, padded deck loungers, and family-friendly oversized games—including giant chess, Connect Four, and beanbag toss—making it a popular spot for multi-generational families to unwind between activities.

Part 2: Permanently Closed / Non-Operational Attractions

If you are building an app database or travel guide, it is helpful to flag these three heavily advertised Park19 features as Permanently Closed so travelers do not expect them onboard:
• Sea Breeze Rollglider (Decks 19–21 — Closed):
	• Planned Concept: Billed as the first electric "hang-gliding" ride at sea, guests were meant to be harnessed into an overhead rail track circling the aft decks at speeds up to 11 mph for a 60- to 90-second flight.
	• Current Status: Permanently closed. Princess Cruises declined delivery from the ride manufacturer due to certification and safety issues.
• Coastal Climb & The Lookout (Decks 19–20 — Closed):
	• Planned Concept: A two-deck vertical obstacle climbing structure ascending from Deck 19 to an open-air 360-degree viewing platform (The Lookout) on Deck 20, paired with dry tube slides returning to Deck 19.
	• Current Status: Permanently closed; dry slides were removed and the upper stairs/structure are sealed off.
• The Net Ropes Course (Deck 19 — Closed):
	• Planned Concept: A harnessed high-ropes adventure course suspended above Deck 19 featuring unstable cargo bridges, climbing nets, and a Z-shaped balance beam.
	• Current Status: Permanently closed and painted over to blend into the superstructure.
