Main fitness centers on Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess are accessible 24 hours a day for self-guided workouts, while staffed studios, outdoor sports courts, and lap pools follow daily operating windows.
Because onboard hours can shift slightly for weather, high winds, or stateroom noise restrictions directly beneath a deck, always confirm exact daily times in the Princess Patter or the MedallionClass app.

Sun Princess Exercise Venues & Hours

• Lotus Fitness Center (Deck 18, Midship).
	• Hours: Open 24 hours (Staffed desk: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM).
	• Short Description: A 24-hour ocean-view gym featuring Precor cardio/strength equipment.
	• Long Description: Overlooking the main Lido pool deck through floor-to-ceiling windows, this complimentary facility houses Precor treadmills with personal screens, ellipticals, adaptive motion trainers, stationary bikes, free weights, kettlebells, a Smith machine, and a Total Gym Elevate core station. While doors stay unlocked 24/7, fitness staff are only on duty during daytime hours.
• Fitness & Wellness Studios (Deck 18, Midship).
	• Hours: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Closed overnight outside scheduled sessions).
	• Short Description: Two dedicated group and recovery studios adjacent to the main gym.
	• Long Description: The Fitness Studio hosts Xponential Fitness classes (Pure Barre, YogaSix, Club Pilates, CycleBar, StretchLab) during scheduled class blocks. The neighboring Wellness Studio offers zero-gravity chairs, personal training, and metabolic assessments by appointment during staffed Lotus Spa hours.
• SkyDeck Sport Court & Track (Deck 19, Aft).
	• Hours: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Track accessible 24 hours; running/court noise restricted overnight).
	• Short Description: An open-air multi-sport court encircled by an elevated outdoor running track.
	• Long Description: Occupying the upper outdoor recreation space, this venue features a 240-meter (790-foot) jogging track where roughly 6.7 to 7 laps equal one mile. Inside the track sits a multi-use recreational court outfitted for basketball, pickleball, tennis, ping pong, and shuffleboard. Equipment check-out and court play close by 8:00 PM to prevent noise from carrying to decks below.
• The Dome & Lido Pools (Decks 17–18).
	• Hours: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Main Lido pools) / 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM (The Dome).
	• Short Description: Multi-level glass-enclosed and open-air pools used for lap swimming and select morning wellness sessions.
	• Long Description: Passengers can check out swim tethers for stationary endurance swimming during pool operating hours, and select morning YogaSix or stretch sessions take place inside The Dome before general lounge crowding begins. Pools close overnight for netting and water maintenance.

Circling the perimeter is an open-air jogging track; while walkers can access the deck anytime, running and ball sports are restricted after 8:00 PM to avoid disturbing cabins on Deck 17.

	• Hours: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Putters and balls available at the Sports Desk until dusk).

	• Long Description: Positioned on the highest aft deck overlooking the sports court, this artificial-turf putting course is open throughout daylight hours for casual recreation and scheduled putting competitions.

Paid Boutique Fitness Classes (All Three Ships)

All three ships host instructor-led classes through Princess Cruises' partnership with Xponential Fitness. Because spaces are capped at roughly 20–26 participants, you must sign up in person on embarkation day at the fitness center desk (they cannot be reserved pre-cruise).
• Pure Barre ($30 / 30 min). A low-impact, full-body workout using the ballet barre, light weights, and mats to tone muscles through small isometric movements.
• YogaSix ($30 / 30 min). Sensory-focused Vinyasa and restorative yoga flows designed for all flexibility levels. On Sun Princess, select morning sessions are hosted inside The Dome.
• CycleBar ($30 / 30 min). High-energy indoor cycling rides set to upbeat playlists, tracking resistance and RPMs.
• Club Pilates ($30 / 30 min). Mat-based Pilates sessions emphasizing core strength, spinal alignment, and controlled breathing.
• StretchLab ($30 / 25–30 min). One-on-one or small-group assisted stretching sessions targeting tight hips, shoulders, and lower backs.
• Boot Camp & TRX Circuit ($30 / 30–45 min). High-intensity interval training (HIIT) and suspension-strap circuits offered on select sea days.
• 1-on-1 Personal Training & Wellness Assessments ($85–$115+ / 60 min). Private training sessions, body-composition/metabolic checks, and posture consultations booked through the fitness staff.

Complimentary Exercise & Active Events (All Three Ships)

Every ship offers a daily rotation of free, drop-in fitness activities led by either the fitness instructors or the cruise entertainment staff (listed daily in the Princess Patter and MedallionClass app):
• Morning Stretch & Detox Stretch (Free / 30 min). Guided full-body flexibility and mobility routines held each morning in the fitness studio or on an open deck.
• Fab Abs & Core (Free / 30 min). A quick, mat-based abdominal and lower-back conditioning class offered on sea days.
• Walk-a-Mile Group Meetups (Free / 30–45 min). Hosted morning or sunset group walks along the outdoor jogging track (or Deck 7 Promenade on Sapphire Princess).
• Poolside Dance & Zumba Fitness (Free / 30–45 min). High-energy Latin dance, line dancing, and cardio routines led by the entertainment team on the Lido pool deck.
• Organized Sports Tournaments (Free / 45–60 min). Staff-run brackets for 3-on-3 basketball, pickleball, volleyball, table tennis, and shuffleboard on the upper sports courts, plus putting competitions on Sky Princess (The Greens) and Sapphire Princess (Lawn Court).
• Passenger vs. Officers Sports Match (Free / 45 min). A staple sea-day event where guests compete against ship officers in basketball, volleyball, or table tennis.
• Fitness & Posture Seminars (Free / 45 min). Educational workshops held in the fitness studio covering topics like Improving Posture & Foot Pain, Eating More to Weigh Less, and Detoxing for Energy.

Beyond the gym and sports courts, Sun Princess, Sky Princess, and Sapphire Princess host several complimentary social dance classes, mind-body sessions, destination-inspired movement workshops, and casual deck challenges throughout the voyage. Most of these are led by the cruise entertainment team rather than spa fitness instructors, meaning they are free and require no advance sign-up.

Social & Cultural Dance Classes (Complimentary)

Hosted daily on sea days in the Piazza, Vista Lounge, Club Fusion, or on the Lido pool deck, these classes offer a high-step cardio alternative to traditional workouts:
• Ballroom & Partner Dance Lessons (45 min). Step-by-step instruction in waltz, foxtrot, tango, cha-cha, and swing led by the onboard dance hosts or production cast.
• Country Line Dancing & Boot Scootin' (30–45 min). Fast-paced group line dances taught in the Piazza or main lounges, often paired with themed country deck nights.
• Salsa, Bachata & Merengue Workshops (45 min). Latin rhythm sessions focusing on footwork and partner turns, typically held in the afternoon before evening live Latin bands perform.
• Destination-Inspired Dance Classes (30–45 min). Depending on the itinerary, guest cultural ambassadors lead regional movement classes such as Hawaiian Hula (Pacific sailings), South American Samba, or Caribbean soca/reggae deck sessions.
• Themed Deck Parties & Nightclub Dancing (60–90 min). High-energy evening dance sessions—such as the Sailaway Party, White & Gold Deck Party, or Silent Disco—held on the Lido Deck, inside The Dome (Sun Princess), or at Skywalkers Nightclub (Sapphire Princess).

Mind-Body & Low-Impact Sessions (Complimentary)

• Morning Tai Chi on Deck (30–45 min). Offered on select voyages (especially longer, Asia-Pacific, or world cruise itineraries), instructors lead slow, flowing martial arts movements on an open deck or in a lounge.
• Guided Meditation & Breathwork (30 min). Quiet seated relaxation and breathing sessions hosted in the morning or late afternoon in a lounge or The Dome.
• Aqua Aerobics / Poolside Stretch (30 min — Itinerary Dependent). While Princess does not schedule daily water aerobics on every sailing, warm-weather Caribbean and Mexican Riviera cruises occasionally feature shallow-water cardio or pool games led by the cruise staff on the Lido deck.

Casual Deck Games, Active Youth Clubs & Recovery

• Active Deck & Poolside Competitions (30–45 min, Free). Light physical games run by the entertainment staff, including bean bag toss (cornhole), deck quoits, golf chipping challenges, water pong, and poolside relay games.
• Youth & Teen Active Programming (Free, Ages 3–17). Camp Discovery (ages 3–12) and The Beach House (ages 13–17) run dedicated teen sports court takeovers, hip-hop dance tutorials, scavenger hunts across the ship's stairwells, and mini-tournaments.
• Post-Workout Thermal Recovery (Paid / Free Sauna Access).
	• Sun Princess & Sky Princess (The Enclave): Features a hydrotherapy pool with cascading rain showers, heated stone loungers, a hammam (Turkish steam bath), and aromatic steam rooms (paid day/voyage pass).
	• Sapphire Princess (Lotus Spa Thermal Suite): Includes two aromatic steam rooms, a dry sauna, and heated ceramic loungers (paid pass, though small complimentary steam rooms are also located inside the locker rooms).
• In-Cabin On-Demand Fitness TV (Free). All three ships provide complimentary fitness, stretching, and yoga routines on the stateroom television system for passengers who prefer private workouts.
