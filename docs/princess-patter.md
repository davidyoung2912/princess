The Princess Patter is the official daily shipboard newsletter delivered to staterooms each evening (and mirrored digitally in the Princess MedallionClass app's JourneyView timeline). Typically formatted as a 4- to 6-page folded booklet, it serves as the comprehensive daily operational guide for guests.
Here is a complete, section-by-section structural outline of everything included in a standard daily edition of the Princess Patter, organized from the front cover to the back page.

1. Front Page: Header, Operational Logistics & Daily Highlights

The front cover establishes the day's identity, maritime conditions, and critical port or sea-day logistics.
• Edition Metadata: Ship name, voyage number, day of the cruise (e.g., Day 3 of 7), full date, and the day’s Port of Call or At Sea status.
• Evening Dress Code: Clearly stated at the top (e.g., Smart Casual or Formal / Dress to Impress).
• Navigation & Weather Forecast: Expected daytime high/low temperatures, general weather conditions, sunrise/sunset times, and sea state/wind notes.
• Port Arrival & All-Aboard Times (Port Days Only):
	• Estimated time of arrival and gangway clearance.
	• All-Aboard Time (typically 30–60 minutes prior to sailing) and scheduled sailaway time.
	• Berth/pier location, local time zone changes (reminders to set clocks forward or back), and tender ticket instructions if anchoring offshore.
• Port Agent & Emergency Contact Information: The name, physical address, and local telephone number of the ship’s port agent ashore (required safety information for guests going independent).
• Featured "Don’t Miss" Spotlights: 3 to 4 editorial callout boxes highlighting the day's flagship production show, guest entertainer, deck party, or enrichment lecture.

2. Inside Pages (Pages 2–3): Chronological Event Schedule ("Today's Events")

The core of the Patter is a minute-by-minute chronological listing of every onboard activity, usually broken down into time-of-day blocks:
• Morning (6:00 AM – 11:59 AM): Fitness classes, stretch/walk-a-mile, The Wake Show broadcast reminders, morning trivia, enrichment lectures, port shopping talks, and youth club morning themes.
• Afternoon (12:00 PM – 5:59 PM): Poolside games, live deck bands, culinary/ice-carving demos, afternoon movies (Movies Under the Stars), art auctions, bingo, dance classes, and affinity/hobby meetups (Bridge, Mahjong, solo travelers).
• Evening & Late Night (6:00 PM – Late): Live lounge music sets (Piazza string quartets, piano bars, jazz trios, pub bands), mainstage theater showtimes (early and late seatings), game shows, comedy club acts, theme parties, stargazing, and nightclub DJ hours.
• Event Metadata Tags: Each listing specifies the exact start time, event title, venue name, deck number, and small icons/notes indicating whether a fee applies ($), if it is part of Discovery at Sea, or if pre-registration is required.

3. Dining & Beverage Guide ("Good Eats & Drinks")

A dedicated grid detailing where and when guests can eat and drink throughout the day, reflecting port-day vs. sea-day adjustments.
• Complimentary Dining Hours: Exact breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, and dinner service windows for each Main Dining Room (noting traditional vs. flexible dining), the Buffet (The Eatery / World Fresh Marketplace), International Café, and poolside grills/pizzerias.
• Casual & Specialty Dining Hours: Operating times and reservation reminders for venues like Crown Grill, Sabatini’s, The Catch by Rudi, Alfredo’s/Gigi’s, and O’Malley’s.
• Daily Culinary Features: Callouts for the evening's Main Dining Room theme, buffet dinner theme, or special pop-up lunches (e.g., British Pub Lunch or Crab Shack).
• Bar & Lounge Hours + Drink of the Day: Opening and closing times for all shipboard bars, happy hour specials, wine tasting times, and the featured Cocktail of the Day and Zero-Proof Mocktail of the Day.

4. Onboard Services, Shopping & Wellness Directory ("Shipboard Hours & Offers")

Usually located on the back page or inside right panel, this section lists operational hours and daily promotions for revenue and guest-service venues:
• Guest Services & Shore Excursions: 24-hour Guest Services desk note, Captain’s Circle Loyalty Desk hours, Future Cruise Sales desk hours, and Shore Excursions desk times.
• Medical Center: Clinic walk-in hours and the 24-hour emergency medical phone number (contacting emergency services from ship phones).
• Boutiques & Galleries:
	• Duty-free shop opening/closing times (noting customs closure while in port).
	• Daily retail promotions (e.g., Captain's Table sidewalk sales, Inch of Gold, jewelry trunk shows).
	• Photo Gallery & Fine Arts Gallery hours.
• Lotus Spa, Fitness Center & Salon: Opening hours, daily port-day or sea-day spa treatment specials, and fitness studio hours.
• Princess Casino: Slot and table game operating hours (noting sailaway opening rules) and daily tournament buy-in times.
• Youth & Teen Centers (Camp Discovery / Firefly Park): Session hours for each age tier (3–7, 8–12, 13–17), port-day supervision sign-up deadlines, and late-night kidsitting rates.
• OceanFront / MedallionNet® Tech Support: Desk hours for Wi-Fi and app assistance.

5. Safety, Customs & Advisories (Footer / Back-Page Notices)

Standardized legal, environmental, and operational reminders tailored to the day's location:
• Local Customs & Agricultural Advisories: Warnings against taking fresh fruit, meat, dairy, or plants ashore in port.
• Environmental & Smoking Policies: Reminders regarding designated smoking areas and strict prohibitions against throwing anything overboard.
• Security & Gangway Requirements: Reminders to carry your OceanMedallion and government-issued photo ID when going ashore, plus security screening procedures upon return.
• Stateroom TV Channel Guide: Listing of onboard channels, including the Bridge Cam, Navigation Map, The Wake Show replay channel, and safety briefing channel.
