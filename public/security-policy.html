Reporting a security problem

This is an unofficial concept app, not a Princess Cruises product. If you find a way to see someone else's data, bypass sign-in, or break the app on purpose, please tell us before telling anyone else.

How to report

Email security@example.invalid (a placeholder until the app has a real owner) with what you found, the steps to repeat it, and how to reach you.

What to expect

  • We will reply within five working days.
  • We will not take legal action against anyone who reports in good faith, avoids other people's data, and does not disrupt the service.
  • Please do not test with real guest data, run automated floods, or publish details before a fix.
